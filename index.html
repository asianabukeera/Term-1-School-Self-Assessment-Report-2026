Executive Summary

Schools demonstrate strong performance in wellbeing, feeding, leadership development and financial accountability. Reading culture remains the most commonly reported challenge. Community involvement in reading and long-term sustainability planning continue to require focused attention.

Pillar 1: Safe Schools

Most schools reported child protection focal persons, reporting channels, counselling support and referral mechanisms. However documentation, periodic review and staff training remain inconsistent across schools.

Performance Score: 85%
Strong awareness exists but implementation systems vary in maturity.

Pillar 2: Wellbeing & WASH

School feeding emerged as one of the strongest programme areas. Most schools reported regular meals, gardens, hygiene promotion, handwashing facilities and health awareness activities.

Performance Score: 88%
School feeding and learner wellbeing are major strengths.

Pillar 3: Reading & Learning

Libraries, reading clubs and reading timetables are increasingly available. However schools repeatedly identified weak reading culture and low community participation in reading activities.

Performance Score: 65%
Reading culture remains the largest system-wide challenge.

Pillar 4: Leadership & Community

Student leadership, stakeholder meetings and collaborative planning have strengthened across many schools. Community participation remains active but uneven.

Performance Score: 78%
Leadership systems are developing faster than community engagement.

Pillar 5: Sustainability & Management

Financial systems are improving through budgeting, finance committees and accountability mechanisms. Income generating activities remain small-scale in many schools.

Performance Score: 72%
Accountability is improving but sustainability remains a concern.

Cross-Cutting Strengths

  • School feeding programmes
  • Teacher collaboration
  • Child protection awareness
  • Financial accountability
  • Student participation structures
  • Leadership development

Cross-Cutting Challenges

  • Reading culture
  • Parent engagement in reading
  • Library utilisation
  • Sustainability planning
  • Career guidance
  • Infrastructure maintenance

Priority Recommendations

  1. Strengthen reading culture across schools.
  2. Increase community participation in reading activities.
  3. Diversify school income-generating activities.
  4. Institutionalise child protection documentation and reviews.
  5. Expand career guidance and entrepreneurship programming.
  6. Improve water systems and maintenance planning.
  7. Increase monitoring of implementation quality across pillars.
